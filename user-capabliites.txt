Role	Sees	Decides
Worker	Own profile, tier and reason, health record, the brief and cards	Check-ins. Whether to contest a reassignment. Whether to share the fitness outcome with the employer



Foreman	Actions for the crew. Never answers or results	Confirms work-rest changes and buddy pairs



Planner	Tiers, work-term reasons and controls for the site	Accepts or declines swaps, with a logged reason. Max 3 reassignment recommendations per shift



Shift manager	The planner's reassignment proposals	Approves a "reassign today" swap (from the tier table in 3.3)



Permit issuer	The permit, crew status as counts, the conditions	Signs, holds or sends back a permit. Can edit drafted conditions



HSE head	Crew and activity aggregates, fitness outcomes, every record	Confirms warning sections, creates actions (CAPA), shares lessons



Occupational health clinician	Health detail and lab trends	Fit, fit with restrictions, or not today. Decides which cohort trends leave the clinic. Has the final word on health



Public health officer	District aggregates of 10 or more	District actions and the summary for the authority



Labour inspector	District and employer aggregates, plus the audit trail	Where to inspect
