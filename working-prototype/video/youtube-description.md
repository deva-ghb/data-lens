## Title

Data Lens: deciding workforce risk before the shift (product demo, 11 min)

## Description

Data Lens helps a busy container terminal decide, before a shift starts, who is fit for which job tonight.
It catches trouble early, and keeps people's health details private.

This demo follows one night at Mirsal Terminals in Abu Dhabi, in July heat.
You meet the eight people who use it, from the roster planner to the clinic, and see every main flow end to end.
Along the way we follow Marco, a lasher, through a shift that goes a little differently than planned.

What you will see:
- A roster sorted into fit, fit with extra controls, and reassign, with reasons in plain work terms
- A one-click job swap that needs a second signature from the shift manager
- A site map that flags places where heat and fatigue are building, with a ten-step warning record
- A worker's phone screen in their own language, with a three-tap check-in
- Permits to work that show crew readiness as counts, never names
- A clinician view where only the clinic sees health detail, and the worker decides what the employer sees
- An evidence page that tests the scoring on ninety days of past data
- An audit trail where every suggestion and every decision is recorded

Mirsal Terminals and everyone in this video are fictional.
All names and data are made up for the demo.
The narration is a computer-generated voice, and the music was composed in code for this video.
